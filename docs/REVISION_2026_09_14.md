## Confirmed rules
Strength caps at 10 per Attack hit; Wisdom owns Hidden Schemes; Wind uses Gale/Pursuit. Temporary Status Cards never become permanent collectibles. Normal rewards offer five distinct choices and at most one acquisition. Permanent decks have **no maximum**; every reward, including Divine/foundation, allows Skip. Realm minimums 12/14/16/18/20 restrict removals, while legitimate smaller decks can advance unchanged. Merchant/Rest upgrades use pure preview then confirmation. Explicit card targets govern all play paths; Scarlet Requiem has no intrinsic healing. English, Simplified Chinese and Vietnamese, numeric/letter/traditional grades, existing art, cross-Path freedom, death, four Divine slots and final Ascension are preserved.

## Tunable values
Flowing Guard cost 1. Internal Injury direct loss 2; Scorched Meridian external hit 2. Cloud boss and reward weighting are initial tuning, not evidence of human satisfaction. Route budget: 10 normal + 3 Elite + 5 Tribulations + 8 utility nodes (26 chosen nodes).

## Implementation assumptions
Numeric grades 1–8 default; optional F/E/D/C/B/A/S/SS or traditional Ding–Tian are presentation only. The catalog has 114 definitions: 83 Dao, 14 Immortal, 8 Divine, 2 Basic, 1 Token and 6 Status; 107 can be permanently owned. Old v1 remains playable through its frozen build. Local rules 0.2 saves continue through an archived snapshot migration, without replaying paid actions or silently starting over. Human editorial review and newcomer sessions remain NOT RUN.
