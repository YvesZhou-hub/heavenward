## How to read this matrix

- **CODE** means a concrete implementation and ordinary access route exist. It does not mean the commercial release gate passed.
- **PARTIAL / OPEN** identifies unfinished implementation, acceptance, or evidence. **FUTURE** is explicitly outside Human Realm gameplay. **DECISION** points to documented interpretations of underspecified rules.
- Implementation paths in tables are under `src/game/` unless explicitly named `Game.tsx`, `Card.tsx`, `Dialog.tsx`, `DeckBrowser.tsx`, or `FieldGuide.tsx` (under `src/components/`). Source locations are preserved file names and one-based section-start lines; the referenced section includes its subordinate requirements.
- **L** means EN/ZH data and/or localized interface implementations are present. Neither language has received certified native-editor approval, and no row using L implies complete visual or assistive-technology acceptance.
- Production art coverage is integrated and registered: 103 dedicated card illustrations, 18 enemies, five summons and one player portrait. `PRODUCTION_ASSET_MANIFEST.json` is `draft: false`; its 127 outputs and stable mappings exist. Exact image-model identity remains unexposed, while final visual, audio and interaction acceptance stays separate from asset coverage.
