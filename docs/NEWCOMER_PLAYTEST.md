# Newcomer playtest — revision 0.2

Status: NOT RUN. No person has been observed in this revision. Bot results are separate diagnostics. Do not narrate the mechanics before play.

## Session setup
Use a fresh browser profile, default English and numeric grades. Let the participant choose Chinese or Vietnamese if preferred. Record consent for any screen/audio recording. Ask them to think aloud only when comfortable. Do not explain the meaning of a keyword unless they ask; record the request first.

## Natural tasks
1. Begin a life, choose two starting techniques, and enter the first encounter.
2. Choose and use a card. Let them discover inspecting, targeting, invalid-drop feedback and End Turn.
3. After a win, compare five rewards and learn one or Skip.
4. Use a Merchant or Rest Site. Observe whether they understand selecting versus confirming an upgrade, unchanged cost, new grade and remaining allowance.
5. Continue through a Tribulation. Observe whether warning, Cloud beasts, Command and the heavy hit lead to different decisions.
6. Switch grade display and language, refresh, and resume the same journey.

## Record without leading
- Time to first intentional play and first completed turn; decision time for each reward/shop/route/upgrade.
- Keyword or icon they inspect, and questions asked before an explanation.
- Whether dynamic damage, individual hits, Path resources and explicit versus natural discard are understood.
- Wrong targets, insufficient Dao Yuan, accidental taps, unclear confirmation or unavailable actions.
- Which cards feel redundant or weak, which combinations feel satisfying, and whether an offered off-Path card solves a real need.
- Fight active duration and total active duration including reading, rewards, shops and route decisions. Separately record pauses/background time. Do not substitute headless execution time.
- Why they defend, race the boss or kill a beast. Do not imply that one answer is always correct.

## Close
Ask: “Which decision mattered most?”, “What was confusing?”, and “Would you choose another run?” Record verbatim where allowed. Compare several participants/builds before treating an isolated outcome as a balance conclusion. Native Vietnamese/Chinese editorial review and assistive-technology review remain separate gates.
