CULTIVATION ROGUELIKE DECKBUILDER
FULL GAME CONCEPT & DESIGN OVERVIEW


==================================================
1. HIGH-LEVEL CONCEPT
==================================================

This game is a run-based tactical cultivation roguelike deckbuilder set in a Chinese xianxia-inspired world.

The player controls a cultivator who begins at a low cultivation realm and gradually grows stronger by:

- building and refining a deck,
- learning techniques from different Dao Paths,
- combining archetypes,
- gaining cultivation progress,
- defeating enemies and Elite enemies,
- surviving Heavenly Tribulations,
- breaking through cultivation realms,
- and eventually ascending to a higher World.

A single run represents one cultivator's life and cultivation journey.

If the player loses any combat, the character dies and the run ends.

There are:

- no checkpoints,
- no mid-run resurrection,
- no traditional open-world exploration,
- and no normal RPG quest loop.

The game is inspired by roguelike deckbuilders such as Slay the Spire and cultivation card games such as Yi Xian, but its main identity comes from the interaction between:

- cultivation realms,
- card grades,
- Dao Paths,
- multiple archetypes within each Path,
- cross-Path synergies,
- Divine Abilities,
- Heavenly Tribulations,
- summons,
- and World Ascension.


==================================================
2. WORLD STRUCTURE
==================================================

The game is planned around multiple Worlds.

WORLD 1 — HUMAN REALM
人界

This is the current development priority.

The player begins at Qi Refining and eventually reaches Spirit Transformation.

The Human Realm progression is:

Qi Refining
→ Foundation Establishment
→ Core Formation
→ Nascent Soul
→ Spirit Transformation
→ Ascension Tribulation

After reaching Spirit Transformation, the player does NOT immediately ascend.

The player continues fighting while at Spirit Transformation, gaining and upgrading high-grade cards and completing the final part of the build.

Only after reaching the final stage and defeating the Ascension Tribulation does the player successfully ascend.

The flow is:

Ascension Successful
→ Human Realm run ends
→ Return to main menu
→ Spirit Realm becomes unlocked


WORLD 2 — SPIRIT REALM
灵界

This is future content.

Spirit Realm is a new run, not a direct continuation of the same Human Realm deck.

The player begins the Spirit Realm at approximately Spirit Transformation level.

The future progression is:

Spirit Transformation
→ Void Refinement
→ Body Integration
→ Great Ascension
→ Final Ascension Tribulation
→ Immortal Realm

After completing Spirit Realm, World 3 would become available.


WORLD 3 — IMMORTAL REALM
仙界

Future design.

It does not need to be defined yet.


IMPORTANT DEVELOPMENT RULE:

For now, development should focus on Human Realm.

Spirit Realm and Immortal Realm are future content.


==================================================
3. CULTIVATION REALMS
==================================================

The full planned cultivation ladder is:

1. Qi Refining — 炼气
2. Foundation Establishment — 筑基
3. Core Formation — 结丹
4. Nascent Soul — 元婴
5. Spirit Transformation — 化神
6. Void Refinement — 炼虚
7. Body Integration — 合体
8. Great Ascension — 大乘

Each major realm also contains four minor stages:

Early Stage
→ Middle Stage
→ Late Stage
→ Peak Stage

At the Peak Stage, the player eventually encounters a Heavenly Tribulation (天劫).

Passing a breakthrough Tribulation allows the player to enter the next cultivation realm.

The final Tribulation of a World instead causes Ascension to the next World.


==================================================
4. CARD GRADES
==================================================

Cards have eight grades corresponding to the eight cultivation realms.

From lowest to highest:

Qi Refining
→ Ding Grade — 丁

Foundation Establishment
→ Bing Grade — 丙

Core Formation
→ Yi Grade — 乙

Nascent Soul
→ Jia Grade — 甲

Spirit Transformation
→ Huang Grade — 黄

Void Refinement
→ Xuan Grade — 玄

Body Integration
→ Di Grade — 地

Great Ascension
→ Tian Grade — 天


The important rule is:

The card grade corresponding to the player's cultivation realm is the NORMAL grade for that realm, not an absolute hard cap.

For example:

Qi Refining:
Normal grade = Ding

The player may still upgrade some cards to Bing.

A player may normally use cards up to ONE grade above the grade corresponding to their current realm.

Examples:

Qi Refining:
- Ding = normal
- Bing = usable above-realm card
- Yi or higher = unusable

Foundation Establishment:
- Bing = normal
- Yi = usable above-realm card
- Jia or higher = unusable

Core Formation:
- Yi = normal
- Jia = usable above-realm card

Nascent Soul:
- Jia = normal
- Huang = usable above-realm card

Spirit Transformation:
- Huang = normal
- Xuan = usable above-realm card


There is currently NO hard limit on how many above-realm cards a player may possess.

Instead, obtaining these cards requires investment and opportunity cost.

Normal card rewards usually give cards matching the player's current realm.

Cards one grade above the current realm are mainly created through:

- Rest Site upgrades,
- Merchant upgrades,
- possibly rare special events later.

This means that if a player manages their HP, money and route very well, they are allowed to become unusually powerful.

==================================================
6. CARD CATEGORIES
==================================================

There are four main card categories:

1. Basic Cards
2. Dao Arts
3. Immortal Arts
4. Divine Abilities


------------------------------
BASIC CARDS
------------------------------

Simple starting cards such as Strike and Defense.

They can have grades and can be upgraded.


------------------------------
DAO ARTS
道术
------------------------------

The main cards used to construct builds.

Dao Arts:

- belong to different Dao Paths,
- support archetypes,
- have card grades,
- can be upgraded,
- form the majority of the player's deck.


------------------------------
IMMORTAL ARTS
仙术
------------------------------

Rare and powerful techniques.

They may represent:

- burst attacks,
- strong defensive techniques,
- short-duration buffs,
- unusual tactical effects,
- powerful combo enablers.

They have card grades and can be upgraded.

They may come from:

- Elite rewards,
- rare combat rewards,
- special events,
- inheritance,
- rare Merchant offers.


------------------------------
DIVINE ABILITIES
神通
------------------------------

Divine Abilities are persistent combat powers.

They exist as cards inside the deck.

The player must:

draw the Divine Ability
→ pay Dao Yuan
→ activate it

After activation, its effect remains active until the end of combat.

The card itself then leaves normal card cycling, usually entering the Exhaust Pile.

Divine Abilities:

- have no card grade,
- cannot be upgraded,
- may be sold,
- are powerful,
- are NOT required to win the game.

A player may deliberately build a deck without using Divine Abilities.


MOST IMPORTANT RULE:

Divine Abilities have only ONE acquisition source:

HEAVENLY TRIBULATIONS.

They cannot normally be obtained from:

- Merchants,
- normal Events,
- inheritance,
- normal combat rewards,
- Elite rewards.

Human Realm supports a maximum of FOUR Divine Ability slots.

The first four major breakthrough Tribulations may each provide one Divine Ability.

The final Ascension Tribulation ends the run and therefore does not need to reward another Divine Ability.


==================================================
7. DAO YUAN
==================================================

Dao Yuan (道元) is the main combat energy resource.

It refills at the beginning of every Player Turn.

Example early progression:

Qi Refining:
3 Dao Yuan

Foundation Establishment:
4 Dao Yuan

Core Formation:
6 Dao Yuan

Higher cultivation realms may increase it further.


Typical card-cost ranges:

Basic Cards:
around 1 Dao Yuan

Dao Arts:
around 1–3 Dao Yuan

Immortal Arts:
around 3–5 Dao Yuan

Divine Abilities:
around 5–6 Dao Yuan


Dao Yuan only exists as a combat resource.

The player does not need to wait outside combat to regenerate it.


==================================================
8. HAND AND CARD PILES
==================================================

At the beginning of every Player Turn:

Draw 5 cards.

Maximum hand size:

10 cards.


Combat uses four main card zones:

- Draw Pile
- Hand
- Discard Pile
- Exhaust Pile


If the Draw Pile becomes empty:

Shuffle the Discard Pile
→ create a new Draw Pile


The Exhaust Pile does NOT automatically return to the deck.

Only special effects can interact with it.


------------------------------
HAND OVERFLOW
------------------------------

If the player's hand is already full and a card attempts to enter the hand:

The card goes to the Discard Pile instead.

This applies to:

- normal Draw,
- cards generated by Skills,
- Token cards,
- Flying Swords,
- other Add-to-Hand effects.


Example:

Hand = 9/10

Generate 2 Flying Swords.

Flying Sword 1:
→ enters Hand

Flying Sword 2:
→ enters Discard Pile


The player does NOT permanently lose the generated card.

The game uses several general deckbuilding keywords.


------------------------------
STRENGTH
------------------------------

Each stack:

+1 damage to every Attack hit

Current planned cap:

5 Strength


Example:

5

Player has:
3 Strength

------------------------------
FORTIFY
------------------------------

Each stack:

+10% Armor gained

Percentage calculations are rounded DOWN.


------------------------------
WEAK
------------------------------

Attack damage dealt:

-25%

Weak does not affect direct Self-Harm.


------------------------------
VULNERABLE
------------------------------

External damage received:

+50%

Self-Harm is not affected.


------------------------------
POISON
------------------------------

At the end of the affected unit's Turn:

Take damage equal to current Poison stacks.

Then:

Poison loses 1 stack.


Example:

5 Poison
→ take 5 damage
→ Poison becomes 4


------------------------------
EXHAUST
------------------------------

The card enters the Exhaust Pile and leaves normal card cycling for the remainder of combat.

Some Refinement Path cards may later interact with the Exhaust Pile.


------------------------------
RETAIN
------------------------------

The card remains in the player's hand when the Player Turn ends.


------------------------------
INNATE
------------------------------

The card is prioritized into the player's opening hand.


------------------------------
DRAW
------------------------------

Draw additional cards.


------------------------------
DISCARD
------------------------------

Move cards from Hand to the Discard Pile.


==================================================
10. DYNAMIC CARD INFORMATION
==================================================

Card values should visually update when combat modifiers change them.

The player should NOT have to mentally calculate hidden damage.

Example:

Strike base damage:
5

Player has:
3 Strength

The card should display:

8 damage

NOT:

5 damage and then secretly deal 8 after being used.


Target-specific previews should also display the expected result whenever possible.

For multi-hit cards:

5 × 4 damage

is better than simply displaying:

20 damage

because many defensive mechanics interact with each hit separately.


==================================================
11. ACTIVE DISCARD
==================================================

Discard is separated into different categories.


1. ACTIVE DISCARD

The card is discarded because of a player or enemy effect.

This CAN activate special Discard-trigger effects.


2. NATURAL END-TURN DISCARD

Cards naturally discarded when the Player Turn ends.

This does NOT count as Active Discard.


3. HAND OVERFLOW DISCARD

A card attempts to enter a full hand and is sent directly to the Discard Pile.

This does NOT count as Active Discard.


Enemy-forced discard DOES count as Active Discard because the discard is caused by an explicit effect.


==================================================
12. DAMAGE CATEGORIES
==================================================

The combat system distinguishes several kinds of HP interaction.


------------------------------
ATTACK DAMAGE
------------------------------

Damage caused by Attack cards.

It may interact with:

- Strength,
- Weak,
- Vulnerable,
- Armor Pierce,
- Sword Intent,
- other Attack modifiers.


------------------------------
EXTERNAL DAMAGE
------------------------------

Real damage that does not necessarily come from an Attack card.

Examples:

- Poison,
- Tidal Domain attacks,
- linked damage,
- certain special effects.


------------------------------
SELF-HARM / DIRECT HP LOSS
------------------------------

Self-Harm is NOT considered normal damage.

It directly removes HP.

Therefore it bypasses:

- Armor,
- Damage Reduction,
- Protective Qi,
- dodge,
- Weak,
- Vulnerable.

This is especially important for Blood Path builds.


------------------------------
SUMMON DAMAGE
------------------------------

Summons use their own offensive statistics.

They do NOT automatically inherit the player's:

- Strength,
- Armor Pierce,
- similar buffs,

unless an effect explicitly says so.


------------------------------
DAMAGE LINK
------------------------------

Damage transferred through a link is still real damage.

Therefore it may be:

- blocked by Armor,
- reduced by Damage Reduction,
- reduced by Protective Qi,
- dodged if appropriate.

Damage Link is NOT Self-Harm.


==================================================
13. DAMAGE CALCULATION
==================================================

The general damage-resolution order is:

Base Damage

→ Attacker modifiers

→ Strength / Armor Pierce / card-specific bonuses

→ Weak

→ Vulnerable

→ Damage Reduction

→ Dodge effects

→ Intercept

→ Protective Qi

→ Armor

→ HP Loss

→ On-Hit / On-Damage / On-HP-Loss triggers

→ Death check


All percentage-based calculations use FLOOR rounding.


==================================================
14. DAO PATH DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
==================================================

Every major Dao Path should support multiple archetypes.

A Dao Path should NOT have only one obvious playstyle.

Each archetype should have:

- a unique gameplay loop,
- its own payoff,
- supporting cards,
- bridge cards connecting it to other archetypes.

Cross-Path combinations are encouraged.

The player is never permanently locked into a single Dao Path.

火道
==================================================

Fire has two main archetypes.


------------------------------
EXPLOSIVE FLAME
------------------------------

Focused on single-target burst damage.

This archetype is especially useful against:

- bosses,

------------------------------
SPREADING FLAME
------------------------------

Focused on multi-target pressure.

Fire damage can spread from one target to other enemies.

Useful against:

- groups,
- bosses with minions,
- summon-heavy enemies.


Bridge cards should allow:

Explosive Flame

Spreading Flame

18. WATER PATH
水道
==================================================

Water has two complementary archetypes.


------------------------------
FLOW
------------------------------

Focused on Dao Yuan management.

Possible effects include:

- restoring Dao Yuan,
- reducing card costs,
- improving Dao Yuan efficiency,
- increasing maximum Dao Yuan.


A Water Divine Ability may permanently increase maximum Dao Yuan for the rest of combat.


------------------------------
TIDAL DOMAIN
------------------------------

This archetype revolves around:

Tidal Momentum


TIDAL MOMENTUM

Certain Water cards generate Tidal Momentum.

Rules:

- no duration,
- planned maximum around 5.


When Tidal Momentum reaches 4:

Finish resolving the current card/effect

→ automatically consume 4 Tidal Momentum

→ summon the Tidal Domain


No separate summon card is required.

No extra Dao Yuan cost is required.


------------------------------
TIDAL DOMAIN
------------------------------

The Tidal Domain is NOT a normal summon.

It:

- has no HP,
- does not occupy a summon slot,
- cannot Intercept damage,
- cannot be sacrificed,
- cannot be eaten by summon-related enemies,
- does not receive normal summon buffs.

It visually exists behind or around the player.


Immediately after Tidal Domain appears:

the player chooses one of three states.


In later turns:

when the player presses End Turn,
the game asks the player to choose a state again.


The Tidal Domain only activates if the player ends the turn with at least:

1 unused Dao Yuan


The unused Dao Yuan is NOT consumed.

It is only the activation condition.


------------------------------
RISING TIDE
------------------------------

At the end of the Player Turn:

Attack the enemy with the lowest current HP.

Damage:

3 + current Tidal Momentum

Then:

+1 Tidal Momentum


This is the resource-building state.


------------------------------
TRANQUIL SEA
------------------------------

At the end of the Player Turn:

Do not attack.

Gain Armor:

3 + (2 × current Tidal Momentum)

Then:

-1 Tidal Momentum


This is the defensive state.


------------------------------
RAGING TIDE
------------------------------

At the end of the Player Turn:

Attack the enemy with the highest current HP.

Damage:

4 + (2 × current Tidal Momentum)

Then:

-1 Tidal Momentum


This is the offensive state.


------------------------------
WATER INTERNAL SYNERGY
------------------------------

Flow:

helps the player manage Dao Yuan efficiently.

Tidal Domain:

rewards the player for leaving at least 1 Dao Yuan unused.

Therefore:

Flow
→ supports
→ Tidal Domain

19. METAL PATH
金道
==================================================

Metal represents two concepts:

Sharpness
and
Hardness


------------------------------
ARMOR PIERCE
------------------------------

Armor Pierce represents Metal's sharpness.

Armor Pierce is a stackable buff.

Each stack:

+1 Attack damage

Current planned maximum:

6 stacks

Armor Pierce has NO duration.


Important rule:

If an Attack hits Armor but causes NO HP loss:

Armor Pierce remains.


If an Attack hit reaches HP:

all Armor Pierce stacks are consumed.


For multi-hit Attacks:

this is checked hit by hit.


Example:

Player has:
4 Armor Pierce

Attack:
3 hits × 3 base damage

Enemy:
10 Armor


Hit 1:

3 + 4 = 7 damage

Enemy Armor:
10 → 3

No HP loss

Armor Pierce remains.


Hit 2:

3 + 4 = 7 damage

3 removes remaining Armor

4 reaches HP

Armor Pierce is consumed.


Hit 3:

only base damage and other remaining modifiers apply.


------------------------------
PROTECTIVE QI
罡气护体
------------------------------

Protective Qi represents Metal's hardness.

Each stack:

reduce every incoming external damage hit by 1


Current planned cap:

5


At the end of the owner's Turn:

Protective Qi is reduced by half.

Round down.


Examples:

5 → 2
4 → 2
3 → 1
2 → 1
1 → 0


Protective Qi is different from Earth Armor.


Example:

Enemy attacks:

5 hits × 5 damage

Player has:

3 Protective Qi


Each hit:

5 → 2

Total:

25 → 10


Against one large hit:

30 damage
→ 27 damage


Therefore:

Earth Armor:
strong general damage absorption

Metal Protective Qi:
especially effective against many small hits


DIVINE ABILITY EXAMPLE:

INDESTRUCTIBLE GOLDEN BODY
不坏金身

==================================================
24. INTERCEPT
==================================================

Summons naturally protect their owner through Intercept.


Each living summon provides approximately:

20% Intercept


Therefore:

1 summon:
20%

2 summons:
40%

3 summons:
60%


When the player receives incoming damage:

calculate the intercepted portion

→ divide that damage between living summons

→ remaining damage continues toward the player


This gives Summoning builds natural defense.


To balance this:

- summon HP should remain limited,
- summon-healing should not be excessive,
- repeated summon cards should only moderately restore or increase summon HP,
- summons remain vulnerable to AoE,
- some enemies may specialize in anti-summon mechanics.

==================================================
26. REFINEMENT PATH
炼道
==================================================

Refinement has two archetypes.


------------------------------
REFINING
------------------------------

Focused on Exhaust.

Exhausting cards can trigger effects such as:

- gaining Armor,
- drawing cards,
- increasing damage,
- healing,
- gaining temporary Strength,
- gaining other resources.


Stronger versions of these effects should belong to Immortal Arts.


------------------------------
FLYING SWORD + REFINING COMBINATION
------------------------------

Flying Swords naturally Exhaust after use.

Therefore:

Sword Flying Sword archetype
+
Refinement Exhaust archetype

creates an intentional cross-Path synergy.


A rare Refinement Immortal Art may:

Select up to 5 Attack cards from the Exhaust Pile

→ return them to Hand


This can allow:

Generate Flying Swords

→ use Flying Swords

→ Flying Swords Exhaust

→ recover up to 5 Attacks

→ launch another wave


This combo can become extremely powerful when prepared correctly.

That is intentional.


------------------------------
RE-REFINEMENT
------------------------------

Re-Refinement temporarily upgrades card grades during combat.

Example:

Ding card

→ temporarily becomes Bing


The temporary grade increase lasts only for the current combat.

After combat:

the card returns to its real permanent grade.


Re-Refinement:

- does not permanently upgrade the card,
- cannot exceed the allowed temporary grade limit,
- does not transform a card into another card.


Card transformation belongs to the Transformation Path.

阵道
==================================================

Formation Path is NOT simply the "Retain Path."


Cards gain special effects when they actually remain in the player's hand through the end of a Player Turn.


Examples:

Retained for 1 Turn
→ gain additional damage

Retained for 2 Turns
→ reduce cost

Retained for several Turns
→ unlock an additional effect


The fantasy is:

prepare the formation

→ maintain it

→ wait

→ activate it at the correct moment

Time Path

==================================================
30. HEAVENLY TRIBULATIONS
天劫
==================================================

Heavenly Tribulations are the main progression gates between cultivation realms.

At the Peak Stage of a realm:

the player eventually faces a Tribulation boss.


Tribulation bosses are PRE-DESIGNED.

They are NOT procedurally generated.


However:

the game can analyze the player's current build and choose an appropriate boss from the existing boss pool.


Possible analysis factors include:

- dominant Dao Path,
- dominant archetype,
- Armor reliance,
- summon reliance,
- burst reliance,
- multi-hit reliance,
- card-type dependence,
- average card power,
- number of above-realm cards.


The boss should test a weakness in the player's build.

The boss should NOT completely invalidate the build.


The goal is:

challenge

NOT

hard counter.


==================================================
31. HEAVENLY OMEN
==================================================

Before entering a Heavenly Tribulation:

the player receives a warning called a Heavenly Omen.


Example:

"Void energy distorts the surroundings.
Defensive techniques may not remain completely reliable."


The warning gives the player information about the type of challenge ahead.

It does NOT reveal the entire boss moveset.


==================================================
32. EXAMPLE TRIBULATION BOSS CONCEPTS
==================================================

------------------------------
CLOUD-VOID BEASTMASTER
------------------------------

Combines:

- Cloud,
- Void,
- Summoning.


Possible mechanics:

- summon Cloud creatures,
- gain defensive effects when minions die,
- share incoming damage with minions,
- gain a one-hit dodge,
- use telegraphed Armor-piercing attacks.


------------------------------
BLOOD-LINK CULTIVATOR
------------------------------

Uses a Damage Link technique.

For a short duration:

Whenever the boss receives damage:

the player receives a percentage of that damage through the link.


The boss still receives the full original damage.


Damage Link is REAL damage.

Therefore:

- Armor can block it,
- Damage Reduction can reduce it,
- Protective Qi can reduce it,
- dodge effects can potentially avoid it.


Damage Link is NOT Self-Harm.


------------------------------
RESTRICTION-SOUL CULTIVATOR
------------------------------

May use effects such as:

- restricting Attack cards,
- restricting Skill cards,
- reducing next-turn Dao Yuan,
- consuming player summons.


A restricted card may still be used.

However:

if the boss survives,
the player may suffer backlash.


If the forbidden card kills the boss:

no backlash occurs.


==================================================
33. DAO ROAD
==================================================

The run uses a semi-hidden branching node map called the Dao Road.


The player normally sees only:

ONE step ahead.


If the road branches:

all immediately reachable nodes are visible.


Nodes beyond that distance remain hidden.


Possible node types include:

- Normal Combat
- Elite Combat
- Unknown Event
- Merchant
- Rest Site
- Inheritance
- Heavenly Tribulation


The goal is:

give meaningful route choices

without allowing the player to plan the entire run from the beginning.


==================================================
34. REST SITE
==================================================

At a Rest Site, the player chooses ONE option.


------------------------------
REST
------------------------------

Heal approximately:

25% Max HP


------------------------------
UPGRADE
------------------------------

Upgrade:

1 card for free


The player cannot:

heal AND upgrade

during the same Rest Site visit.


Rest Site upgrades may allow a card to become one grade above the player's current normal realm grade.


Reducing Rest Site upgrades to one is intentional.

It limits rapid power growth through opportunity cost rather than through a hard arbitrary restriction.


==================================================
35. MERCHANT
==================================================

The Merchant can:

- sell Dao Arts,
- rarely sell Immortal Arts,
- buy cards from the player,
- upgrade cards.


The Merchant NEVER sells Divine Abilities.


The player may sell up to:

3 permanent deck cards per Merchant visit.


The deck cannot fall below the current minimum deck size.


Divine Abilities may also be sold.


The Merchant currently allows approximately:

2 paid card upgrades per visit.


The second upgrade should cost noticeably more than the first.


Again:

the goal is opportunity cost,
not an arbitrary hard power limit.


==================================================
36. DECK SIZE
==================================================

Current planned deck ranges:


Qi Refining:
Minimum 12
Maximum 18


Foundation Establishment:
Minimum 14
Maximum 22


Core Formation:
Minimum 16
Maximum 26


Nascent Soul:
Minimum 18
Maximum 30


Spirit Transformation:
Minimum 20
Maximum 34


The player cannot sell cards below the minimum deck size.


The purpose is to prevent extremely tiny looping decks while still allowing meaningful deck trimming.


==================================================
37. CARD REWARD LOGIC
==================================================

Card rewards are NOT completely random.


The reward generator may consider:

- current cultivation realm,
- current Dao Paths,
- archetypes already present,
- bridge-card opportunities.


Example:

The player already uses many Flying Sword cards.

The game may slightly increase the probability of:

- Flying Sword support,
- Sword Intent bridge cards,
- related Sword Dao Arts.


This should only be a WEIGHTING bias.

It should NOT force the player to remain inside the same build.


The player must still be able to pivot into new Paths or archetypes.


==================================================
38. NORMAL CARD-GRADE REWARDS
==================================================

Normal card rewards should usually match the player's current cultivation grade.


Example:

Spirit Transformation player:

most normal card rewards
→ Huang Grade


Lower-grade cards may still occasionally appear.

Cards one grade above the player's current normal grade should NOT commonly appear from normal rewards.


Above-realm cards should mainly come from deliberate investment such as:

- Rest Site upgrades,
- Merchant upgrades.


==================================================
39. DEATH
==================================================

If the player loses ANY combat:

the run ends.


No checkpoint.

No revival.


The Run Summary may display:

- run duration,
- highest cultivation realm reached,
- normal combats won,
- Elite enemies defeated,
- Tribulations cleared,
- bosses defeated,
- cards obtained,
- Immortal Arts obtained,
- Divine Abilities obtained,
- currency earned,
- main Dao Paths,
- main archetypes,
- cause of death.


==================================================
40. META PROGRESSION
==================================================

The game should NOT use permanent combat-stat progression.


No permanent:

- +HP
- +damage
- +Strength
- +Dao Yuan
- similar direct stat bonuses


Instead:

meta progression unlocks CONTENT.


Examples:

- new cards,
- new characters,
- new events,
- new bosses,
- new starting options,
- cosmetics,
- card backs,
- lore,
- challenge achievements.


A History / Collection system can record discoveries across runs.


==================================================
41. DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
==================================================

The player should become powerful because they made good decisions.

The game should NOT constantly weaken the player simply because the player is performing well.


Strong builds should be controlled through:

- opportunity cost,
- card rarity,
- money,
- HP,
- setup requirements,
- deck consistency,
- enemy design,
- Heavenly Tribulations.


The game should allow the player to occasionally create an extremely powerful or nearly broken run.

That is part of the fun of a roguelike deckbuilder.


The game should avoid hidden systems such as:

"The player has too many strong cards, so secretly reduce Merchant appearance rates."

or:

"The player's build is strong, so secretly remove Rest Sites."


Strong play should be rewarded.

43. CURRENT DEVELOPMENT PRIORITY
==================================================

The complete game concept contains:

- multiple Worlds,
- many Dao Paths,
- many archetypes,
- many card grades,
- many Tribulation bosses.


However:

THE CURRENT PRIORITY IS HUMAN REALM.


Spirit Realm should be developed later.


Even Human Realm should initially be implemented through a smaller playable prototype.


The most important question right now is:

"Is the combat system actually fun?"


Before expanding the game dramatically, the prototype should prove that:

- playing cards feels satisfying,
- archetypes feel different,
- cross-Path combinations are interesting,
- Dao Yuan management matters,
- deckbuilding decisions matter,
- Heavenly Tribulations are exciting,
- combat information is readable,
- powerful combos feel rewarding.
